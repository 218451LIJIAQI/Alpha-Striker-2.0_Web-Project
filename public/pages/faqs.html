Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Click Sign In in the top-right corner and choose Create Account. Enter your Full Name, Email, and Password, accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and submit to sign in immediately.
On the Sign In page, click Forgot Password? to request a 6-digit verification code for your registered email. Signed-in members can also switch to the Current Password tab to update their password directly.
Open Movies in the top navigation, filter by genre or search by title, and click Book Now. Choose your Theater, Date, Showtime, and up to 10 seats (using mouse or arrow keys), then click Proceed to Payment.
Open Theaters in the top bar, search by theater name or street, or filter by format (IMAX, 4DX, VIP, Dolby Atmos, RPX). Click View Theater Details to inspect amenities, 3D sightlines, and live showtimes.
Visit the Offers page to copy or claim an active promo code, then click Apply on checkout. Reserving 5 or more seats automatically unlocks a 30% group discount on the ticket subtotal!
Registered members receive member-exclusive promo codes, save digital tickets in their wallet, and cancel reserved seats online anytime before the screening.
Major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex with valid future MM/YY expiry), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. Your authoritative booking receipt and digital e-ticket are generated immediately upon confirmation.
Click Tickets in the upper-right header bar to inspect your reserved row, seat, zone, and barcode, or click Print / Save PDF.
Cancellations & Refunds: Signed-in members can cancel reserved seats directly on the Tickets page, releasing the seat immediately back into auditorium inventory.
