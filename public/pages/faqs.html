Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I register a new account?
Click Sign In in the top-right corner and choose Create Account. Enter your Full Name, Email, and Password (at least 6 characters), accept the Terms & Privacy Policy, and submit to sign in automatically.
2. What if I forget my password?
Click Forgot Password? on the Sign In page, enter your registered email address, click Send Code to receive a one-time 6-digit verification code (valid for 5 minutes), and set your new password. Signed-in users can also update their password directly using their current password.
3. How do I search for and book a movie?
Browse Movies in the top navigation, filter by genre or search by keyword, and click Book Now. Choose your Theater, Date, Showtime, and Seats (A1–H12; Rows D & E are Prime VIP), then click Proceed to Payment.
4. How does the 30% Group Discount work?
Selecting 5 or more seats in a single booking automatically applies a 30% discount to the ticket subtotal on both the seat picker and checkout pages.
5. How can I redeem a promo code?
Visit the Offers page and click Copy or Use on Booking. At checkout, click Apply next to the Promo Code box to verify expiration and movie eligibility before paying.
6. Which payment methods are accepted?
Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay are supported at checkout, with a flat $2.50 service fee per order.
7. How do I access, print, or save my e-ticket as PDF?
Open Tickets in the top bar to view your digital passes and barcodes, then click Print / Save PDF on any ticket.
8. Can I cancel a reserved seat?
Signed-in members can cancel their own reserved seats directly from the Tickets page. Immediately after cancellation, the seat is released back to the auditorium map.
9. How do I preview auditorium sightlines in 3D?
Click 3D Auditorium View on the Seat Selection page or Theater Details page to inspect viewing angles across Rows A–H.
