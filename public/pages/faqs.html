Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I register a new account?
- Click Sign In in the top-right corner and choose Create Account.
- Fill in your Full Name, Email, and Password, then agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Click Create Account. You’ll be automatically logged in and redirected to the Movies page.
2. What if I forget my password?
- On the Sign In page, click Forgot Password?
- Enter your registered email address and verification code, then choose a new password.
3. How do I search for and book a movie?
- Click Movies in the top navigation.
- Use the search bar or genre filters to find your desired film and click Book Now.
- Select your Theater, Date, Showtime, and Seats, then click Proceed to Payment to complete your booking.
4. How do I find theaters near me?
- Click Theaters in the top menu.
- Search by theater name or street, or filter by format (IMAX, 4DX, VIP, Dolby Atmos, RPX).
- Click View Theater Details to see the address, amenities, and live showtimes.
5. How can I use a promo code or coupon?
- Go to the Offers page to view all active promotions.
- Click Copy or Use on Booking on any offer card.
- On the checkout page, apply the code in the Promo Code field to redeem your discount.
6. What is the membership program and how does it work?
Earning Points: Registered members earn points on every ticket purchase.
Tier Levels: Points accumulate to upgrade through Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers.
Member Benefits: Unlock extra discounts, priority seating, and concession perks.
7. Which payment methods are accepted?
- Major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay
Payment is processed instantly; your digital e-ticket is available immediately after confirmation.
8. How do I access or print my e-ticket?
- Click Tickets in the upper-right header bar.
- View your reserved row, seat, zone, and barcode, or click Print / Save PDF.
9. Can I cancel or change my booking?
Cancellations: Signed-in members can cancel reserved seats directly on the Tickets page.
Rescheduling: Cancel the existing seat reservation and book your preferred new showtime.
