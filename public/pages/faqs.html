Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I register a new account?▾
- Click Sign In in the top-right corner and choose Create Account.
- Fill in your Full Name, Email, and Password, then agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
- Click Create Account. You’ll be automatically logged in and redirected to the Movies page.
2. What if I forget or want to change my password?▾
- On the Sign In page, click Forgot Password? and request a 6-digit verification code for your registered email.
- Signed-in members can also switch to the Current Password tab to update their password directly.
3. How do I search for and book a movie?▾
- Click Movies in the top navigation.
- Use the search bar or genre filters to find your desired film and click Book Now.
- Select your Theater, Date, Showtime, and Seats (up to 10 seats per order), then click Proceed to Payment.
4. How do I find 4DX, IMAX, or VIP theaters near me?▾
- Click Theaters in the top menu.
- Search by theater name or street, or filter by format (IMAX, 4DX, VIP, Dolby Atmos, RPX).
- Click View Theater Details to inspect the address, amenities, 3D sightlines, and live showtimes.
5. How can I use a promo code or group discount?▾
- Go to the Offers page to copy or claim an active promo code.
- On checkout, enter the promo code in the Promo Code box and click Apply.
- Booking 5 or more seats automatically unlocks a 30% group discount on the ticket subtotal!
6. What is the membership program and how does it work?▾
Member Perks: Registered members can receive member-exclusive promo codes, save digital tickets in their wallet, and cancel seats online anytime before the screening.
7. Which payment methods are accepted?▾
- Major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex with valid future MM/YY expiry)
- PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay
Your authoritative booking receipt and digital e-ticket are generated immediately upon confirmation.
8. How do I access or print my e-ticket?▾
- Click Tickets in the upper-right header bar.
- View your reserved row, seat, zone, and barcode, or click Print / Save PDF.
9. Can I cancel or refund my booking?▾
Cancellations & Refunds: Signed-in members can cancel reserved seats directly on the Tickets page.
Rescheduling: Cancel the existing seat reservation and book your preferred new showtime.
