Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Welcome to CineTix (Alpha Striker 2.0). We are committed to protecting your personal data while delivering a reliable cinema booking experience.

1. Information We Collect

a) Account & Profile Data

  • Required: Display name, email address, and bcrypt-hashed password.
  • Optional: Support inquiry contact details when voluntarily submitted.

b) Booking & Transaction Data

  • Selected movie, theater location, screening date/time, and seat coordinates.
  • Payment method selection and redeemed promotional codes (raw card numbers are never stored on our servers).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Reserve auditorium seats, generate digital tickets, and prevent double-bookings.
  • Authenticate account access, process password resets, and honor ticket cancellations.

3. Data Security & Retention

Account passwords are stored exclusively as salted bcrypt hashes, session cookies use HttpOnly protection, and booking records can be cancelled directly from your Tickets wallet.
