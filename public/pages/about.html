Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Alpha Striker 2.0 began with a simple idea: to create a seamless bridge between moviegoers and theaters. Founded in 2015 by a group of film enthusiasts and tech innovators, we set out to transform the way people experience cinema.

What started as a small startup with a handful of partner theaters has now grown into a trusted movie ticketing platform serving movie lovers across partner locations.

Our journey is driven by a passion for engineering clarity and a deep love for cinema — making movie discovery, 3D sightline previews, and seat reservation effortless.
