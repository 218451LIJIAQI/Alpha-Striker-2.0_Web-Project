Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Alpha Striker 2.0 began with a clear goal: build a direct, trustworthy bridge between moviegoers and theaters without hidden booking hurdles.

What started with a handful of partner auditoriums has grown into a dedicated movie ticketing platform serving cinema enthusiasts across flagship formats.

Every detail — from 3D auditorium sightline previews to automatic 5+ seat group discounts — is engineered around clarity and guest comfort.
