Our Story

From Vision to Reality

Alpha Striker 2.0 began with a simple idea: to create a seamless bridge between moviegoers and theaters. Founded by film enthusiasts and software engineers, we set out to transform how audiences discover and reserve screenings.

From interactive 3D sightline previews to transactional group seat reservations, every workflow is crafted to make booking tickets as enjoyable as the feature film itself.
