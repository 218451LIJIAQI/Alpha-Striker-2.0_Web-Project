📚 Interactive Walkthroughs

1. Booking Your First Ticket & Previewing 3D Sightlines
Open Movies, choose a film, select your preferred theater, date, and showtime, and click 3D Auditorium View to inspect sightlines from Rows A–H before reserving up to 10 seats.
2. Viewing, Printing & Exporting Digital E-Tickets
Sign in to your account and open My Tickets. Click Print / Save PDF on any ticket card to print a clean single-ticket pass with barcode.
3. Redeeming Promo Codes & 30% Group Savings
Claim an offer on Offers (such as STUDENT30) to auto-fill it at checkout, or select 5+ seats on Seat Selection to trigger an automatic 30% group discount.
4. Managing Reservations & Password Security
Cancel individual seats anytime from My Tickets, or update your password from Password Security using either email verification or your current password.
