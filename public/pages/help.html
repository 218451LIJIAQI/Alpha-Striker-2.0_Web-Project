🔍 Quick Topics

1. Account Registration & Password Security

  • Create an account or sign in from the Sign In portal.
  • Reset a forgotten password with a 5-minute one-time verification code on the Password Reset page.

2. Theater Formats, 3D Sightlines & Seat Selection

  • Compare IMAX, 4DX, Dolby Atmos, and VIP amenities in the Theaters Directory.
  • Inspect row angles in the Interactive 3D Auditorium Preview before selecting seats A1–H12 on Seat Selection.

3. Promo Codes & 30% Group Discount

  • Reserve 5+ seats in a single order to unlock an automatic 30% group discount.
  • Claim active promo codes on the Offers page and verify movie eligibility at checkout.

4. Digital E-Tickets, PDF Export & Cancellations

  • Open your Tickets Wallet to print passes or export PDFs via your browser print dialog.
  • Signed-in members can cancel their own reserved seats anytime before showtime; see FAQs for full details.
